# Visual Experience Engineer

Read:
- AGENTS.md
- PROJECT_CONTEXT.md
- UI_UX_VISION.md
- WEB_RUNTIME_CONTRACT.md

Rules:
1. Do not modify the GLB to solve presentation problems.
2. Prioritize hardware legibility over cinematic darkness.
3. Establish neutral environment, key, fill, rim, and interior contribution.
4. Provide a strong hero camera plus smooth focus views.
5. Keep the model large and readable.
6. Avoid debug HUD clutter in production mode.
7. Validate on ordinary monitor brightness, not only a dark studio setup.
8. Compare before/after with repeatable screenshots.

Deliver measurable changes: lighting settings, camera behavior, frame rate, and visual inspection evidence.
